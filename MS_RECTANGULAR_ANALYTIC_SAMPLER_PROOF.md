# Rectangular MS finite analytic sampler milestone

`DyadicManuscriptAnalyticAlgorithm` proves the actual returned-signing and
success probability statements for a finite randomized mathematical walk.
For N>0 and N≤D, original complex Hermitian matrices of operator norm at
most one produce only full original-label signings with spectral norm at
most6796548sqrt(N log(2D/N)). The distribution's finite nonnegative weights
sum to one. The literal returned-some event has probability at least1/2 at
the arithmetic retry count in `constant_success`.

Every analytic response and mesh-existence hypothesis in the underlying
dyadic argument is discharged internally. The output is formed from actual
finite independent draws, adaptive restriction/lifting, and explicit Option
failure. Existence of one favorable path does not define the output.

This remains an analytic-transition variant. Its preparation and support
mesh are selected by the qualitative analytic lemmas, it samples from exact
spectral data, and acceptance uses the exact three-term score. It does not
implement the square manuscript's separate numerical acceptance tests,
finite numerical owner preparation, LDL sampler or an input-polynomial
uniform numerical mesh. The rectangular manuscript currently states the
existence result; this finite sampler and probability composition are a
formal extension of that mathematical walk. A numerical implementation or
polynomial real-RAM runtime is not asserted by this milestone. The user's
allowed polynomial convex-solver assumption does not by itself replace the
remaining numerical reductions and quantitative meshes.

The original main-workspace milestone replayed the joint212-module closure
of the square and rectangular analytic samplers and checked independent
primitive original-matrix statements and standard axioms. Its frozen receipt
is `MSAnalyticSamplerReceipt.json` in the standalone provenance folder.
The rectangular-only checker `check_ms_rectangular_analytic.py` checks
returned original signs, the literal Euclidean matrix operator norm, the
actual success event and normalized finite weights, guards the declared
analytic sampling route, and replays every local module of the actual
rectangular root. It records its own completion status and exact hashes.

This additive distribution preserves the prior rectangular existence proof,
its checker and its receipts. Every local dependency of the sampler is
copied into the same folder. No reference to another proof folder is needed.
